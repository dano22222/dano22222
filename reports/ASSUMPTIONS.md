# Part 3 methodological decisions

1. The main compulsory experiment is NO3, 2024, hourly ERA5. Request `models=era5` explicitly, not the API default blend. Use UTC, 8784 hours in the leap year, 2 m air temperature in degrees Celsius and precipitation in mm per hour. Reanalysis is modelled weather, not a direct station observation.
2. The assignment simultaneously says single location, sample 1000 locations and plot average weather. Interpret one location as the unit of each request; the required regional experiment averages all 1000 uniformly sampled locations with equal weight. A one-point pilot is labelled separately and cannot stand in for the regional result. No partial regional mean is silently published.
3. Download official NVE electricity polygons and NVE administrative polygons through their public ArcGIS service, retaining the source GeoJSON and exporting full shapefile bundles. This is a programmatic alternative to clicking Download in NVE Atlas. The original GeoJSON retains long attribute names that shapefile truncates.
4. Sample uniformly in equal-area EPSG:3035, with deterministic seed 320 plus the sorted region index, then transform to longitude and latitude. Rejection sampling accounts for polygon holes and disconnected components. Coastal administrative boundaries can include marine areas; `cell_selection=land` is explicit. The spatial mean is an approximation, not a reservoir-capacity-weighted or catchment-runoff-weighted estimate.
5. Important metadata discrepancy: current NVE `HentOmråder` identifies VASS1 southeast, VASS2 west, VASS3 mid, VASS4 north. The historical reservoir response currently contains only VASS1–3. Therefore VASS3 must not be labelled as the whole north. Administrative proxies are VASS1=RS+RØ, VASS2=RV, VASS3=RM, VASS4=RN. The assignment's combined RM+RN north polygon is retained separately as PRECIP_NORTH and also receives 1000 points. These proxies do not reproduce precise hydrological boundaries. This limitation is visible in the app and notebook.
6. SPC applies an orthonormal DCT-II. Frequencies below or equal to 1/cutoff_days form a smooth baseline. Subtract it, estimate full-year residual median and Gaussian-consistent MAD, then add residual limits back to the baseline to obtain boundaries on the original precipitation scale. Default cutoff 30 days and multiplier 3. Residuals are not plotted. The assignment's SATV wording in the precipitation section is interpreted as seasonally adjusted precipitation variations. Skewness, zero rain and autocorrelation prevent interpreting these as calibrated Gaussian tail probabilities. Zero MAD is reported explicitly, without replacing it by ordinary standard deviation.
7. LOF uses standardized temperature and its one-hour difference, 48 neighbours, 1% contamination. The first difference is defined as zero. This detects unusual temperatures or changes; contamination is an assumed fraction and does not establish measurement errors. LOF does not establish causes, and scaling does not remove all seasonal effects.
8. STL uses a single area's complete weekly filling fraction series, annual period 52, seasonal smoother 13, trend smoother 105 and robust=True. A 52-week year approximates the calendar year. Require at least two cycles; use the available multi-year history rather than just 2024. Missing weeks and duplicate observations cause an explicit failure instead of silent interpolation.
9. Spectrogram uses exactly that weekly series, a Hann window of 156 weeks, overlap 130 weeks, linear detrending within each segment and density scaling. Frequencies are cycles per 52-week year. Display 0–6 cycles/year for readability; frequency resolution is 52/156 cycles/year. Colour shows 10 log10 power with a 1e-15 floor only for logarithmic display.
10. No bulk download starts automatically in Streamlit. The explicit CLI needs `--approved`; the app offers a one-point pilot only after a click. Cache all successful point requests and stop on HTTP 429, preserving work for a later run. Do not evade provider quotas. No paid service is enabled. The real run reached an hourly quota after 957 complete point series; the resume runner waits for the next hour rather than changing endpoints or identities.
11. Preserve the original Streamlit app URL and entry point. Develop on part3-development. The user subsequently authorized all necessary completion steps, including push, merge into main and updating the existing deployment. GitHub origin/main contains merge 8dbf70b from part2-development, verified after fetch. The local main was behind and is not used as an authoritative starting point. Streamlit Cloud is configured to use Python 3.11 to match the tested local runtime; its previous setting was 3.14. The URL is unchanged.
12. Existing local edits and historical deliverables remain available. Changes to existing files have backups. Historical notebooks are also copied into notebooks/; originals are not moved or deleted. Python comment tokens added or edited for Part 3 use English lowercase and no hyphens, as requested.
